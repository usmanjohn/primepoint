Based on the user's own tale `Zero gravity.docx` (repo root, 8 parts, written in Uzbek).
Kept: King Zero, the twin Ones, the signs as toys, the ÷0 tantrum, the stick that becomes
the number line, «kasr» as the fraction bar's name, the root cage, the irrational khanate,
the Real Numbers empire. Changed only where the draft would teach something false (§3).

## 1. The one idea

**Every episode, a new kind of number is born — because the old ones could not do
something.** The Ones cannot undo each other → zero and the minus side. They cannot share
fairly → fractions. They cannot measure a square's diagonal → √2. That is how mathematics
really grew, so the fairy tale never has to lie, and every episode ends with a problem the
kingdom cannot yet solve — which is the hook.

Two laws for every scene:

1. **It is mathematically true.** A metaphor may simplify; it may never mislead. If a pupil
   took the scene literally into an exam, they must still be right.
2. **It is funny.** At least one laugh per scene, and **the joke IS the maths**: a character
   is funny *because of* its property (the twins vanish when they hug *because* 1 + (−1) = 0).
   Kind, family-friendly, Uzbek in flavour — mahalla, toʻy, choyxona, bozor. Nobody is mocked
   for who they are; numbers are mocked for what they do.

Audience: grades 5–7 first (the fairy-tale register), written so a parent laughs too.

## 3. What was changed from the draft, and why

1. **Division by zero (ep. 3) — the user's scene, made exact.** The Ones jump on sleeping
   Zero with ÷ and ask: «Bitta boʻlish uchun nechta sen kerak?» (1 ÷ 0). Zero tries to
   answer: he shrinks to a tenth → ten of him are needed; to a hundredth → a hundred; to a
   thousandth → a thousand. He runs further and faster towards the horizon — **to the right**.
   Then −1 jumps on him and he runs just as madly **to the left** (1 ÷ (−0,001) = −1000). The
   draft's «goh chapga, goh oʻngga yugurar» is literally the true graph of 1/x. He never
   arrives, and the two sides do not even agree — so **there is no answer**. The sparks flying
   to the horizon become **Cheksizlik**: a direction, not a number. Law of the kingdom:
   **«Noʻlga boʻlib boʻlmaydi.»**
2. **«Ratsional» comes from Latin *ratio* = nisbat**, not from a Russian word. The fraction
   folk call themselves **«Nisbat xalqi»** — made of a ratio of two integers.
3. **7 ÷ 3 = 2,333… is still rational.** Its 3s *repeat*, so it is tamed: «2,(3) deb yozamiz».
   The true dividing line comes in ep. 7–8: √2 and π have digits that **never repeat**. This
   kills the classic misconception "endless decimal = irrational".
4. **The number line is ordered by size.** The draft's "evens below, odds above" stays only as
   a parade picture. Zero calling himself even is **true** (0 = 2 × 0) and stays.
5. **π gets its own entrance (ep. 8).** It is not a root: π is the secret of every circle, the
   *ratio* of circumference to diameter — yet never a ratio of two integers. A number named
   after a ratio that refuses to be rational is the best irony in the series.
6. **× and ÷ "born" from + and −** stay as the Ones' *legend*; the Haqiqatda box tells the real
   history.
7. **The draft's last law** («manfiylar ildizga yaqinlashmasin») becomes the Season-2 hook: a
   voice from *off* the line says «√(−1)…». Season 2 = a second, vertical axis.

## 4. Episode structure (site and video share it)

**Site (Corner reading, ~500 words, Uzbek):**
1. cold open — a funny line in the first sentence;
2. the problem the kingdom cannot solve;
3. the comic attempt that fails;
4. the new number is born; it is the solution;
5. the cliffhanger;
6. **«Haqiqatda»** box — the real maths + real history, every fact sourced;
7. 2–3 questions;
8. an `open_question`;
9. «Keyingi qismda…» line.

**Video (Flow, 6–8 scenes × 8 s):** the same beats without the Haqiqatda box. The comedy is
**physical** (Veo animates actions better than wordplay). It ends on the cliffhanger
image, and the last line is «Davomi — powerty.uz da».

## 5. Season 1 — the ten episodes

### Ep. 1 — «Noʻl va egizak Birlar»
- **Math:** zero as the middle; opposites; +1 and −1; 1 + (−1) = 0.
- **Story:** Noʻl rules a perfectly empty plain — calm, fair, and bored. Quyosh gets the name
  «musbat», Oy «manfiy». Zero makes a 1 for Quyosh; Oy, jealous, wants one too; Zero makes
  a second 1, and to tell the twins apart gives each a sign to carry forever.
- **Comedy:** the twins' first hug — *pop!* — both vanish; only Zero stands there. Oy faints.
  They reappear when they let go. Zero, wiping sweat: «Quchoqlashmanglar! Har safar yoʻq
  boʻlib qolasizlar!» (Running gag for the whole season.)
- **Guard:** −1 is not "bad" — it is the opposite (Minusjon: «salbiy emas, manfiy!»).
- **Hook:** the twins find a box of strange toys: «+», «−»… and something else.
- **Haqiqatda:** zero as a number with rules — Brahmagupta, India, 628; negative numbers as
  "debts" in the same book; in China red counting rods for positive and black for negative
  numbers. Al-Xorazmiy (c. 820, Bagʻdod) — his book on the Indian numerals carried the
  decimal system **and zero** westward *(source: `_stories_matematika_olami_01_03.py`)*.

### Ep. 2 — «Ishoralar oʻyini»
- **Math:** + and − as moves; a + 0 = a; a × 0 = 0; × as repeated +.
- **Story:** The twins play with the toys. They tip the «+» on its side — it becomes «×» and
  everything it touches multiplies (the legend). Zero joins in.
- **Comedy:** Zero discovers his superpower and horror: everyone he multiplies vanishes into
  him. «Voy, kechirasiz… yana koʻpaytirib qoʻydim-a.» Adding himself, on the other hand,
  changes nobody: «Qoʻshilaman — hech kim sezmaydi. Koʻpaytiraman — hamma yoʻqoladi. Bu
  qanaqa hayot?!»
- **Guard:** a × 0 = 0 and a + 0 = a — kept exact.
- **Hook:** the twins jump on the «−» toy until two dots fly off it — «÷». Zero is asleep…
- **Haqiqatda:** the signs are young: + and − first printed in 1489 (Widmann); × in 1631
  (Oughtred); ÷ in 1659 (Rahn). *(dates to be re-checked before writing)*

### Ep. 3 — «Nolga boʻlish» (the user's centre-piece)
- **Math:** why 1 ÷ 0 has no answer; 1÷0,1 = 10, 1÷0,01 = 100, 1÷0,001 = 1000; from the
  negative side −10, −100, −1000; infinity is a direction, not a number.
- **Story:** §3 point 1, exactly. The twins drop «÷» on sleeping Zero. He tries to answer,
  shrinks, and runs right — further, faster. −1 jumps: he runs left. Sparks fly to the
  horizon and something is born there that can never be reached: Cheksizlik.
- **Comedy:** Zero sprinting across the plain, crown flying off, shouting numbers as he
  goes («O-o-on! Yu-u-uz! Mi-i-ing!»). Cheksizlik's first line, from the horizon:
  «Yetib keldim!» — camera zooms — he is still on the horizon. «…Hali yoʻldaman.»
- **Law:** «Noʻlga boʻlib boʻlmaydi» — Zero bans himself from ever standing under ÷.
- **Hook:** shaken, Zero builds a wall of new numbers between himself and the horizon:
  2, 3, 4… 100… and the new numbers do not want to obey.
- **Haqiqatda:** for centuries even great mathematicians got this wrong — Brahmagupta wrote
  0 ÷ 0 = 0; in the 12th century Bhaskara II called n ÷ 0 "infinite". Today: undefined.
  *(re-check before writing)*

### Ep. 4 — «Tayoq va saf»
- **Math:** the number line; order by size; positives right, negatives left; even and odd;
  0 is even.
- **Story:** the numbers fight over who stands where. Zero nearly beats them with his staff —
  then lays it on the ground: one mark, one number. Order by size.
- **Comedy:** the parade. Ikki, as drill sergeant: «Juft-juft safga turinglar!» The odd
  soldiers: «Bizda juft yoʻq — biz toqmiz!» The evens complain that both of Zero's wings
  (±1) are odd; Zero: «Men ham juftman!» — «Kim bilan?» — «Oʻzim bilan: 0 + 0!»
- **Guard:** the "evens below, odds above" parade is shown as a parade only; the line itself
  is ordered by size.
- **Hook:** «Joyimiz tor!» — every number wants more land.
- **Haqiqatda:** the number line as a drawing is about 340 years old (John Wallis, 1685).
  Zero is even because it divides by 2 with no remainder. *(re-check)*

### Ep. 5 — «Oraliqdagi yerlar»
- **Math:** halves, quarters; between any two numbers there is always another (density).
- **Story:** the numbers ask for the empty land between the marks. Zero draws a mark halfway,
  then halfway again…
- **Comedy:** a bazaar of land: «Ikki bilan uch orasidan bir yarim sotix bering!» Every time
  the land "runs out", Zero halves it again — the bazaar never closes. A dalloll (broker):
  «Yer tugamaydi, aka, tugamaydi!»
- **Hook:** the numbers turn on Zero: «Egizaklar quchoqlashsa doim sen paydo boʻlasan —
  bizdan foydalanyapsan!» Zero, hurt, apologises and gives everyone every toy, including «÷».
- **Haqiqatda:** between any two fractions you can always fit another — their average.

### Ep. 6 — «Nisbat xalqi»
- **Math:** division makes fractions; the fraction bar; 7 ÷ 3 = 2,333… repeats → 2,(3);
  every integer is n/1, so every integer is rational.
- **Story:** the numbers divide each other. Some answers are whole, some are not; the
  not-whole ones become a new folk — «Nisbat xalqi».
- **Comedy:** Uch — the toʻy host who will not give back the microphone: «…uch, uch, uch…»
  for days. «Kimdir mikrofonni olsin!» Zero notices the 3s just *repeat* — «Xoʻp, 2,(3) deb
  yozamiz, bas.» The user's pun: Zero «umrbod shu chiziqning *kasriga* qoldim» — and the bar
  is named «kasr chizigʻi». Then the Nisbat folk's coup: they put a 1 under every integer —
  «Besh — bu 5/1. Demak sen ham bizdansan!» Overnight the whole kingdom is rational.
- **Guard:** endless + repeating = still rational.
- **Hook:** «Ammo bitta *lekin* bor edi…»
- **Haqiqatda:** *ratio* (lotincha) = nisbat → «ratsional». Every repeating decimal is a
  fraction; every fraction gives a decimal that ends or repeats.

### Ep. 7 — «Ildiz qafasi»
- **Math:** squares and square roots; √25 = 5; √2 is not a fraction (its digits never
  repeat); the number line had holes.
- **Story:** the numbers invent towers (squares) and cellars (roots). 25 goes down and comes
  out as 5; 100 as 10. Then 2 goes down to see — and cannot come out. No whole number, no
  fraction squares to exactly 2.
- **Comedy:** Ildiz-Ikki introduces himself at the gate: «Ismim 1,41421356…» — the guard is
  still writing at sunset; «pasportga sigʻmayapti». The rational folk do not recognise him.
- **Guard:** √2 is a perfectly real length — the diagonal of a 1 × 1 square. It is not
  "broken", just not a ratio.
- **Hook:** Zero exiles the caged ones and forbids negatives near roots. Far away, in the
  *holes* of the line, the exiles start talking.
- **Haqiqatda:** the legend of Hippasus (called a legend) and the short proof that √2 is not a
  fraction; √2 ≈ 1,41421356.

### Ep. 8 — «Doiraning siri»
- **Math:** π = circumference ÷ diameter for every circle; π is not a ratio of integers;
  its digits never repeat.
- **Story:** every wheel, plate, tandir and dome in the kingdom hides the same number. An old
  scholar who has measured them all arrives: Pi.
- **Comedy:** invited to every circle, Pi starts every speech «Doiraning sirini aytsam…
  3,14159265358…» and the whole hall falls asleep. The irony lands: «Ismim nisbatdan —
  lekin men Nisbat xalqidan emasman.»
- **Hook:** Pi joins the exiles as vizier.
- **Haqiqatda:** Archimedes trapped π between 3 10/71 and 3 1/7; that π is irrational was
  proved in 1761 (Lambert). *(re-check)*

### Ep. 9 — «Irratsional xonlik»
- **Math:** irrational numbers as a whole people; e cameo (growth).
- **Story:** the exiles found a khanate in the holes of the line: Ildiz-Ikki is khan, Pi
  vizier, and E, the merchant whose purse keeps swelling. They send Pi as envoy: «Biz ham
  haqiqiy sonlarmiz.»
- **Comedy:** the envoy's speech never ends (π again); E keeps trying to sell Zero compound
  interest («Foiz ustiga foiz!») mid-negotiation.
- **Hook:** Zero must decide — are they *real*?
- **Haqiqatda:** e ≈ 2,71828 from compound interest (Jacob Bernoulli, 1683). Links the shelf's
  existing story «Foiz ustiga foiz: pul qanday oʻsadi». *(re-check)*

### Ep. 10 — «Haqiqiy sonlar imperiyasi»
- **Math:** rational + irrational = real numbers; every point of the number line is a number;
  the line has no holes left.
- **Story:** the two khanates forgive each other and found the Empire of Real Numbers along the
  **sonlar oʻqi**. Zero no longer rules — but stands in the middle, honoured by all.
- **Comedy:** the coronation feast (osh for every number); the twins hug at the table — *pop* —
  «Yana-ya?!» — everyone laughs.
- **Season-2 hook:** night; Zero looks up — *above* the line, where nothing should be, a
  small voice: «√(−1)… meni ham qabul qilasizlarmi?»
- **Haqiqatda:** the real numbers fill the line completely; the number that will open the
  vertical axis is called *i* — Season 2.
