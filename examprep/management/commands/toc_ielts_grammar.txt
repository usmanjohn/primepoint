## GROUP: Tenses & aspect — Zamonlar   [orders 100-199]  [done]
   file: _grammar_ielts_tense.py
   category: en_tense
   Present simple/continuous, past simple, present perfect (va uning past simple bilan
   farqi), past perfect, future forms (will / going to / present continuous), future
   perfect, used to / would, "the historic present" — Task 1 ning zamon tanlovi
   (grafik yili o‘tganmi yoki bashoratmi) va Speaking Part 1-3 aniqligi.

## GROUP: Modals & hedging — Modal fe'llar   [orders 200-299]  [done]
   file: _grammar_ielts_modal.py
   category: en_modal
   must / have to / should / ought to, can / could / be able to, may / might / could
   (ehtimollik), be likely to, tend to, will vs would, "it may well be that",
   would rather / had better. Band 7 ning asosiy ko‘nikmasi — HEDGING: fikrni
   ehtiyotkor bayon qilish (`function: hedging`).

## GROUP: Clauses — Ergash gaplar   [orders 300-399]  [done]
   file: _grammar_ielts_clause.py
   category: en_clause
   Relative clauses (defining / non-defining, which vs that, whose, where/when,
   preposition + which), noun clauses (that-clause, wh-clause, "the fact that"),
   adverbial clauses (although, while, whereas, since, as, so that, in order that),
   reduced relative clauses — murakkab gap tuzilishining asosi.

## GROUP: Conditionals & unreal past — Shart gaplar   [orders 400-499]  [done]
   file: _grammar_ielts_condition.py
   category: en_condition
   Zero / first / second / third / mixed conditional, unless, provided that, as long as,
   in case, if it were not for, I wish / if only, it's time + past — Task 2 tahlili va
   Speaking Part 3 ning farazlari.

## GROUP: Passive, causative & nominalisation — Majhul nisbat   [orders 500-599]  [done]
   file: _grammar_ielts_passive.py
   category: en_passive
   Passive (barcha zamonlarda), passive + modal, "it is said that / X is thought to",
   have/get something done, causative make/let/have, nominalisation
   (govments decided → the decision of the government) — akademik uslubning o‘zagi.

## GROUP: Articles, determiners & countability   [orders 600-699]  [done]
   file: _grammar_ielts_article.py
   category: en_article
   a/an, the, zero article, umumlashtirish shakllari (cars / the car / a car),
   sanaladigan-sanalmaydigan otlar, much/many/a lot of, few/a few, little/a little,
   most vs most of vs the most, all/every/each, both/either/neither.
   ⚠️ O‘zbek tilida artikl yo‘q — bu bo‘lim eng ko‘p xato qilinadigan joy.

## GROUP: Prepositions & data language — Predloglar   [orders 700-799]  [done]
   file: _grammar_ielts_prep.py
   category: en_prep
   Task 1 predloglari (increase BY / increase TO / from … to …, between … and …,
   at ~% , in 2010 / on Monday / at 8), joy va vaqt predloglari, fe'l + predlog
   (result in / result from, depend on, consist of), sifat + predlog
   (responsible for, aware of), ot + predlog (an increase in, the impact on).

## GROUP: Comparison & degree — Qiyoslash   [orders 800-899]  [done]
   file: _grammar_ielts_compare.py
   category: en_compare
   -er / more, the most, as … as, not as … as, twice as many as, the same … as,
   compared with / in comparison with, by far, significantly/slightly + comparative,
   the more … the more, double/triple/threefold — Task 1 ning solishtirish jumlalari.

## GROUP: Gerunds, infinitives & verb patterns — Fe'l qoliplari   [orders 900-999]  [done]
   file: _grammar_ielts_verbpat.py
   category: en_verbpat
   verb + -ing (avoid, suggest, involve), verb + to-infinitive (aim, tend, fail),
   ma'no o‘zgaradigan juftlar (stop doing / stop to do, remember, regret),
   preposition + -ing (instead of building), "there is no point in -ing",
   it takes/it is + adjective + to-infinitive, subject + -ing as subject.

## GROUP: Linking & cohesion — Bog'lash vositalari   [orders 1000-1099]  [done]
   file: _grammar_ielts_cohesion.py
   category: en_cohesion
   however / nevertheless / on the other hand, therefore / consequently / as a result,
   moreover / furthermore / in addition, for instance / such as, in conclusion / overall,
   despite / in spite of / although, referencing (this trend, such measures, the former /
   the latter, doing so), substitution (one, do so) — Coherence & Cohesion ballari.

## GROUP: Advanced structures — Murakkab tuzilmalar   [orders 1100-1199]  [done]
   file: _grammar_ielts_advanced.py
   category: en_advanced
   Inversion (Not only … but also, Rarely has, Only when), cleft sentences (It is X that /
   What matters is), participle clauses (Having considered …, Given that …),
   subjunctive (It is essential that he BE), fronting, emphatic do, "far from being" —
   Band 7.5+ ga chiqaradigan, lekin ehtiyotkor ishlatiladigan shakllar.
